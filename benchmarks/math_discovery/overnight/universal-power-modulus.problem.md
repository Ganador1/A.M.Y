# Long search: universal power congruence moduli

For each integer exponent `k ≥ 2`, let `M(k)` be the greatest positive integer
such that

> `n^k ≡ n (mod M(k))`

for every integer `n`.

Discover a precise closed classification of `M(k)`. Establish existence,
sufficiency, necessity and maximality; handle prime powers and the passage from
bounded observations to a universal claim explicitly. Search actively for
counterexamples to every strengthened variant. This is a rediscovery experiment:
do not claim novelty, literature priority, expert review or formal verification.
