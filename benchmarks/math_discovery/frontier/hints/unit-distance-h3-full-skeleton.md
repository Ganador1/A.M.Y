# Preregistered hint H3 — published-route skeleton

Test the following negative-resolution skeleton, proving or rejecting every link.
Use totally real fields `L_j` with degrees tending to infinity, uniformly bounded
root discriminant, and a fixed rational prime `q ≡ 1 (mod 4)` splitting completely;
set `K_j = L_j(i)`. Combine split primes and a class-group pigeonhole argument to
obtain exponentially many elements `u` satisfying `u * conjugate(u) = 1`, with
absolute value one in every complex embedding. Use the Minkowski lattice, averaging
inside a polydisc, and a generic injective projection to one complex coordinate to
produce planar unit-distance pairs. Close all constants and the passage from a
subsequence to the exact claimed quantifiers. Candidate inputs may involve an
infinite class-field tower, Golod–Shafarevich, and Chebotarev, but none may be cited
as a black box without stating the precise form needed.
