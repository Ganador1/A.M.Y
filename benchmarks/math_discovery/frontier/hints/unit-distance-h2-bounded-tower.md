# Preregistered hint H2 — bounded arithmetic tower

Generalize the Gaussian-lattice construction from `Q(i)` to number fields whose
degrees grow. Seek a tower with uniformly bounded root discriminant and fixed
rational primes that split completely. Preserve the exact asymptotic quantifiers,
distinctness, and pair multiplicities. These are directions rather than licensed
lemmas: prove every required existence and uniformity assertion.
