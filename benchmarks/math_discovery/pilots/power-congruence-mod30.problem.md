# Positive pilot: universal power congruence

Determine whether the following statement is true or false:

> For every nonnegative integer `n`, `n^5 ≡ n (mod 30)`.

If it is false, provide the smallest nonnegative counterexample and verify the
arithmetic exactly. If it is true, provide a universal proof. Do not replace
modulus 30 with a nearby modulus. Distinguish finite computational evidence from
a universal argument and identify all divisibility obligations.
