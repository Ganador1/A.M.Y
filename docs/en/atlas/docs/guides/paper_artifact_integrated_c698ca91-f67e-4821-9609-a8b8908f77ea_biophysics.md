**Abstract**

This report presents the results of a study that evaluated the hypothesis that evidence aggregation on a scientific platform can influence the quality and diversity of the information presented. Although the hypothesis was not confirmed, the analysis revealed significant deficits in the quantity and quality of the available evidence.

**Introduction**

The quality and diversity of scientific information are fundamental for the advancement of knowledge and informed decision-making. In this context, the scientific platform has emerged as an important tool for the exchange and evaluation of evidence. However, the quantity and quality of the evidence available on these platforms can vary significantly. This research focuses on evaluating the hypothesis that evidence aggregation on a scientific platform can influence the quality and diversity of the information presented.
