**Abstract**

This report presents the results of an investigation evaluating the effect of functionalized nanoparticle concentration on neurite outgrowth in neural tissue engineering. It was found that an increase in the concentration of functionalized nanoparticles is associated with a significant increase in neurite outgrowth length. These findings may have important implications for the development of therapies to treat neurological diseases and brain trauma.

**Introduction**

Neural tissue engineering is an emerging area that seeks to develop technologies to repair or replace damaged neural tissues. Neurite outgrowth is a crucial process in the formation and function of the nervous system, and its stimulation may be beneficial for the treatment of neurological diseases. Recently, functionalized nanoparticles have been developed that can be used as signaling agents to stimulate neurite outgrowth.
