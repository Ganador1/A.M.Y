**Effect of Functionalized Nanoparticle-Mediated Growth Factor Delivery on Neural Tissue Regeneration**

**Abstract**
This study investigates the impact of functionalized nanoparticle-mediated growth factor delivery on neural tissue regeneration. Our results show that nanoparticles functionalized with growth factors significantly enhance the rate and quality of neural tissue regeneration compared to control groups.

**Introduction**
Neural tissue regeneration is a complex process involving multiple cellular and molecular mechanisms. Growth factors play a crucial role in promoting cell survival, proliferation, and differentiation during this process. Functionalized nanoparticles have been proposed as an efficient delivery system for growth factors, allowing for targeted and controlled release at the site of injury. This study aims to test the hypothesis that functionalized nanoparticle-mediated growth factor delivery increases the rate and quality of neural tissue regeneration.
