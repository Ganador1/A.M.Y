<a id="mejoras-de-eficiencia-detalladas"></a>
## Detailed Efficiency Improvements

<a id="1-fastvpinns-aceleración-tensorial"></a>
### 1. **FastVPINNs: Tensor Acceleration**
- **Reduction**: 100x in median training time
- **Benefit**: Improved scalability for complex geometries
- **Application**: Partial differential equations in irregular domains

<a id="2-pikans-redes-kolmogorov-arnold"></a>
### 2. **PIKANs: Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks**
- **Improvement**: Separable architectures for better efficiency
- **Benefit**: Better capture of complex spatial hierarchies
- **Application**: High-dimensional Fokker-Planck equations

<a id="3-optimización-de-pérdidas-adaptativas"></a>
### 3. **Adaptive Loss Optimization**
- **Technique**: Adaptive weights based on residuals
- **Benefit**: Faster and more stable convergence
- **Application**: Problems with multiple scales

<a id="4-muestreo-guiado-por-energía"></a>
### 4. **Energy-Guided Sampling**
- **Method**: Adaptive sampling strategies
- **Benefit**: Better computational efficiency
- **Application**: Complex fluid dynamics
