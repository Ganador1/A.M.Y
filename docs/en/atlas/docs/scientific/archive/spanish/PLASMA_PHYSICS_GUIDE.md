## ⚛️ Overview

The **Plasma Physics Service** of AXIOM META 4 is an advanced simulation system for plasma physics and nuclear fusion, equivalent to the computational capabilities of projects such as ITER, JET, and national laboratories. It implements complete models of magnetohydrodynamics (MHD), two-fluid equations, and magnetic confinement configurations for fusion energy research.
