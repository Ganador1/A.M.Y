# A.M.Y 1.1.0rc1 — evidence-aware execution

Release candidate prepared on 20 September 2026. No tag, public release, DOI or external attestation is implied by this document.

## Changes

Persistent receipts and mission-scoped memory retain prior checked experiments in decisions and reflections. Execution success is separated from scientific validity; exhausted goals and failed dispatches no longer serve as successful scientific outcomes. Recovery has bounded failure budgets, and reflection has its own output budget.

The response parser recognizes complete final decisions, distinguishes decision and reflection schemas, rejects ambiguous or truncated output, and records JSON selection. A live regression in the first revision was preserved and corrected using real provider-response fixtures.

Per-run execution evidence retains source/configuration identities, tool artifacts and failures. Memory checkpoints can store reversible deltas. Selected H₂, SSH and population-model tools provide separately checkable certificates. These changes improve auditability within explicit boundaries; they do not establish universal coverage or externally authenticated provenance.

The worker now honors the separate Atlas root passed by the installed runtime; a real subprocess test checks this path. Local encrypted credentials are excluded from the tracked release tree while retained on the operator machine.

Installed distributions include a portable English profile, preferred when no local config.yaml exists. Existing checkout configurations and explicit --config paths retain precedence.

## Evaluation

| Batch | Sessions | Quantitative closures | Verified measurements | Provider calls |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Initial five-model comparison | 10 | 8 | 30 | 75 |
| First parser revision, regression retained | 4 | 0 | 3 | 25 |
| Corrected parser, both GLM models | 4 | 4 | 12 | 33 |
| Clarified H₂ contract, both GLM models | 2 | 2 | 6 | 15 |

The five models were DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, GLM 5.3, GLM 5.3 Flash, Qwen 3.5 397B, and DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813. Twenty execution chains verified; eighteen existing experiment journals verified and two sessions produced no experiments. There were 25 truncated responses among 148 calls, including reflection. These are versioned calibration batches, not interchangeable independent trials.

An ambiguous distance field in the H₂ assessment was corrected to mean the distance of the minimum-energy sample. Both final sessions passed their first synthesis attempt. This is evidence for fixing the interface, not a demonstration of mathematical inability in a model or a general causal performance estimate.

## Research and attribution

The exact autocorrelation witness is packaged separately for review. Reproduced fluid proofs, local combinatorial exclusions and known SSH effects are not advertised as new global discoveries. Algorithms, repairs and protocols written by Codex are distinguished from decisions executed by native AMY; the maintainer reviews and owns the release claims.

## Limitations

Long-duration autonomous reliability remains unresolved. Some tools require optional libraries or have restricted evidence grades. Local hashes are not external signatures. The wheel is a runtime component; Atlas remains a separate installation.
